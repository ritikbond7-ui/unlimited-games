Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

EV Rush ("we", "this site") is a small free browser game website. This policy explains, in plain language, what data is collected when you play and how it is used.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, or any personal details to play the game. The game itself stores only one thing on your device: your best score, saved in your browser's local storage so the game can show it on your next visit. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us.

2. Cookies

This site may use cookies — small files stored by your browser — for basic site operation and, in future, for advertising. You can delete or block cookies at any time through your browser settings; the game will still work.

3. Advertising (Google AdSense)

This site plans to display advertisements served by Google AdSense and its partners. Google may use cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. These are third-party cookies: they are set by Google, not by us, and are governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising technologies page.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. In the European Economic Area and the UK, you may also be shown a consent notice before advertising cookies are used.

4. Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly analytics (or Google Analytics in future) to understand aggregate, anonymous usage — for example, how many people visit the game page. Such tools may set their own cookies as described in their respective privacy policies. We do not use analytics data to identify individual visitors.

5. Children's privacy

EV Rush is a general-audience arcade game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data security and retention

Because we do not operate accounts or collect personal data from players, there is no player database to breach or retain. Any data held by third parties (Google's advertising cookies, analytics providers) is retained according to their own policies.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, when advertising is actually enabled on the site — the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at ritikbond49@gmail.com or via the Contact page.
